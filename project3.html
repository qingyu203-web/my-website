PROJECT 03

Documentary & Video Production

Documentary Post-production Video

A short observational documentary interviewing community influencers, exploring how individuals inspire others and create positive change at a local level.

Documentary cover

This documentary project focuses on interviewing community influencers to understand how their actions, ideas and leadership shape local communities. Through face-to-face interviews and observational footage, the film captures personal motivations, community contributions and the real-world impact of grassroots work.

I completed pre-production planning, location scouting and interview scheduling for this documentary. During production, I used handheld camera work, close-up portraits and natural sound recording. In post-production, I edited interview footage, structured the narrative, and applied colour grading and background music to establish a consistent tone.
