PROJECT 04

China & Ukraine Cross-Cultural Recipe Book

Cultural Research Visual Design Illustration

A cross-cultural visual recipe project comparing shared ingredients and different cooking philosophies between Chinese and Ukrainian traditional cuisine.

Cross-cultural recipe book cover design

This collaborative cultural design project explores food similarities and cultural differences between China and Ukraine. Although the two countries share many identical core ingredients such as flour, potatoes, cabbage, and seasonal grains, their cooking logic, rituals, and symbolic meanings are completely different.

The core of this project is visual cultural comparison: identical ingredients represent shared human daily life, while different cooking methods reflect unique local history, climate, customs, and cultural beliefs. For example, both cultures make stuffed dumplings, yet they carry different festival meanings, shapes, and eating traditions.

Within this project, I took primary responsibility for all food illustrations and the cover design. I created all hand-drawn visual elements, which soften cultural boundaries and present cross-cultural communication through a warm, inclusive visual language.
